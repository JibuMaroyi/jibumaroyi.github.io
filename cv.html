May 2026 – present

Fatima Fellowship

Predoctoral Fellow · Remote

Compared FP16 and INT8 inference in LLaVA-Med on VQA-RAD through a paired pilot of 50 questions, examining memory use, latency and individual answers.

Pilot completed; full evaluation paused. Answer formatting and scoring need revision before a reliable comparison of answer correctness.

What the pilot clarified

The pilot used the same images and questions across inference conditions: 40 closed questions and 10 open questions. Initial exact-match scoring compared full model responses with short reference answers, making the planned analysis of correct-to-incorrect changes unreliable.

Memory and latency measurements describe this pilot configuration, rather than a general result about quantization. My current interest in how vision-language models use visual evidence is a separate research direction.
