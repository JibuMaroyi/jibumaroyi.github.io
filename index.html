Hi, I’m Benjamin

Computer science · AI research / Bukavu, DR Congo

I’m a computer science graduate based in Bukavu, DR Congo. I worked on AfiaFirst-TB, a chest X-ray analysis prototype, and contributed to a published study of antibiotic resistance at Bukavu Provincial General Referral Hospital.

My current interests focus on efficient vision-language models and how they use visual evidence. When a model gives a correct medical answer, I want to understand how much it depends on the image, and what changes when we compress the model or its visual tokens.

Selected work

2025

AfiaFirst-TB

Undergraduate thesis · Research prototype

A CPU-based chest X-ray classification prototype developed with Destin Biringanine, exploring support for tuberculosis screening in resource-limited settings. Built with PyTorch and MobileNetV2.

Preprint
Project details & reported result

The project reported an AUC of 0.952 on public chest X-rays. This is a research-dataset evaluation; clinical validation has not been established. The thesis received 18/20 and the Faculty of Sciences’ best final-year project award at UCB.

2026

Antibiotic resistance in Bukavu

Published · Co-author

Helped digitise 1,801 bacterial-isolate records from hospital registers and contributed to cleaning, analysis and visualisation for a retrospective study, published in Infection and Drug Resistance.

2026

LLaVA-Med / VQA-RAD

Fatima Fellowship · Exploratory pilot

A paired FP16/INT8 inference pilot on 50 questions, comparing memory use, latency and individual answers in a medical vision-language model.

What the pilot clarified

Both inference modes received the same image and question. The pilot exposed limitations in comparing full responses with short reference answers. Full evaluation is paused while answer formatting and scoring are reviewed.
